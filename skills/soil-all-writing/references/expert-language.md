# Expert language and academic naturalness

## Define the target correctly

Aim for prose that an experienced domain expert could defend: precise terms, explicit comparison conditions, economical reasoning, proportionate claims, and genre-appropriate confidence. Do not aim to imitate statistical signals of “human text” or to defeat a detector.

Expert prose can be conventional and repetitive. A standard, patent claim, method, or classification description may need controlled repetition and fixed syntax. Naturalness never overrides unambiguity, legal scope, normative force, evidence, or reproducibility.

## Build an author style sheet

Use only the author’s own or authorized samples. Record observable choices:

- directness and typical paragraph purpose;
- sentence and paragraph length ranges, without forcing random variation;
- first-person and passive-voice preferences by section;
- favored transitions and punctuation;
- terminology, abbreviations, spelling variety, and citation placement;
- typical hedging and strength of interpretation;
- degree of methodological explanation and audience assumptions;
- recurring weaknesses to correct rather than imitate.

Do not fabricate quirks, opinions, humor, emotional reactions, or deliberate disorder. These are inappropriate in most scientific and regulatory genres.

## Diagnose language at five levels

1. **claim:** Does the sentence say exactly what the evidence permits?
2. **information:** Is the topic clear and is new information placed where readers can process it?
3. **cohesion:** Are referents, contrasts, causes, and qualifications explicit without mechanical connectors?
4. **sentence:** Are agency, verb, modifiers, scope, and punctuation controlled?
5. **lexicon:** Are terms, collocations, register, and language variety correct?

Repair higher levels before polishing words.

For Chinese soil-science and adjacent natural-science prose, apply `domain-register-and-word-control.md` after terminology decisions. Reject management-consulting, internet-operations and vague administrative substitutes when the sentence should name a scientific object, variable, method, condition or responsibility. In particular, do not let “口径”“闭环”“赋能”“抓手”“对齐”“颗粒度”等词 pass as professional merely because they are common in institutional prose. Use the context-aware lexicon so legitimate technical senses such as “沉淀反应”“生态系统”“矩阵效应”和排版中的“两端对齐” are not damaged.

## Remove common machine-like weaknesses only when present

- inflated importance and promotional adjectives;
- vague attribution without a source;
- generic “challenges and future prospects” paragraphs;
- empty participial tails or significance clauses;
- repetitive meta-discourse and chat residue;
- forced triples, mirrored “not only…but also” constructions, and ornamental contrasts;
- synonym cycling for the same scientific referent;
- paragraph openings and conclusions that repeat a template;
- excessive abstract nouns when a precise agent and verb are known;
- broad words such as “impact”, “mechanism”, “complex”, or “significant” without a defined meaning.

Do not ban individual words or punctuation globally. “However”, passive voice, three-item lists, nominalization, and hedging can be correct. Flag patterns and functional misuse, not tokens in isolation.

## Prefer expert moves

- Name the object, comparison, scale, condition, and uncertainty.
- State why a method or comparison is needed, not that it is “important”.
- Tie novelty to a verified gap and a concrete capability.
- Separate observation, interpretation, alternative explanation, and implication.
- Use specific limitations and boundary conditions.
- Let paragraph structure follow the scientific move rather than a fixed sentence count.
- Retain discipline-standard phrasing when it is the clearest formulation.

## Review in two passes

Pass 1 checks science, genre, and logic. Pass 2 checks rhythm, concision, cohesion, and author voice. Re-run the protected-element and terminology audits after Pass 2 because small stylistic edits can still corrupt meaning.

Pass 2 must also run the domain-register audit. A natural-sounding sentence still fails if its vocabulary belongs to business promotion, internet operations or empty administrative rhetoric rather than the named discipline and genre.

## Use only the controlled naturalness claim

Do not describe the result as “top-expert”, “better than human”, “publication-ready”, “purely human-authored”, or universally “undetectable” based on self-assessment. When zero residual machine-like features are required, follow `naturalness-assurance.md`. Use its exact scoped assertion only after the locked artifact passes independent discipline and language review; never turn that assertion into a provenance claim.
